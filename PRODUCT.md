## Platform

web

## Users

Audience and ownership are not specified. Visitors can browse the site's Home, About, and Contact pages.

## Product Purpose

The site presents its existing About and Contact information alongside a pickleball-court scene on the Home page. Success means visitors see a looping, decorative pickleball animation and can still reach the existing pages.

## Positioning

The Home page's animated pickleball court is the site's distinctive experience. No broader positioning has been established.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a static HTML and CSS project.
- The Home experience includes a decorative pickleball that bounces around a pickleball court like a DVD logo.
- The animation is not a game and has no click or camera controls. It pauses when offscreen or the tab is hidden and becomes static when reduced motion is preferred.
- The About and Contact pages remain part of the site.

## Brand Commitments

The existing site uses the name AAA and English-language copy. The user specified a green background for the pickleball court.

## Evidence on Hand

The project contains six images under `images/`. No verified customer claims or testimonials are established.

## Product Principles

- Keep the court animation decorative and lightweight.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Preserve access to the existing About and Contact pages.
